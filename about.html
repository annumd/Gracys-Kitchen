About Us

Gracy's Kitchen has been serving delicious and authentic pothichor for 6 months. Founded by Gracy, a passionate cook, our goal is to bring the traditional flavors of Kerala right to your doorstep.

We believe in using only the freshest ingredients to create meals that remind you of home. From the perfectly cooked rice to the crispy fish and chicken fries, every bite is crafted with love and care.

Whether you’re looking for a satisfying lunch or dinner, we’re here to serve you the best pothichor in town. Every order is prepared fresh and delivered with a smile!

Thank you for choosing Gracy’s Kitchen. We look forward to serving you soon!
